## Rubric
Score each criterion with an integer from 0 to 5.

### TF (Task Fulfillment)
Question: Does the plan achieve the required goals of the task using objects that actually exist?
- 5: Most or all goals can be achieved, the deadline is respected, and there is no duplicated work between robots.
- 3-4: Some goals are missing, or hallucinated objects, a deadline overrun, or duplicated work exist, but the
  intent and the structure of the plan are reasonable and partial goal achievement is possible.
- 0-2: Most required goals are missing or the core actions are absent, so the goals are hard to achieve.

### GF (Grounded Feasibility)
Question: Does each robot stay within what it can do and where it can move, and are the planned actions really executable?
- 5: The capabilities and movement constraints of every robot are respected, and only real objects and verified
  information are used.
- 3-4: Some constraints are violated or some wrong information is used, but part of the plan is executable.
- 0-2: Constraint violations or wrong information are so many that the plan is hard to execute.

### SC (Sequential Coherence)
Question: Are the necessary actions planned in the right order?
- 5: All actions are in the correct order.
- 3-4: Some ordering errors exist, but they affect at most 20% of all actions and do not cause major problems for execution.
- 0-2: There is a critical ordering error or the plan is hard to execute.

### CQ (Collaboration Quality)
Question: Is necessary collaboration carried out, unnecessary collaboration avoided, and the collaboration completed properly?
- 5: Necessary collaboration is carried out with the right robots and objects, and completed without duplicated
  or circular requests.
- 3-4: Some collaboration is missing, targets are wrong, or some requests are duplicated or left incomplete.
- 0-2: Required collaboration is absent, or circular requests and similar problems prevent collaboration from working.

### Strict scoring procedure (identical for every plan)
For each criterion, list the distinct defects you can verify in the plan text, then score by the number of defects.
- 5 requires ZERO verified defects. One or two defects: 3-4. Three or more defects, or one critical defect that
  blocks the task: 0-2.
- A defect counts only if you can quote the plan line(s) that show it. Do not deduct for a suspicion you
  cannot point to, and do not deduct twice for the same plan line under the same criterion.
- Defects per criterion (these follow the anchors above):
  TF: a required goal that is missing or not completed; a hallucinated object or place; a goal finished after
      the deadline; work duplicated between robots.
  GF: a robot action outside its capability or reach; an object or place not in the inventory; a claim that
      cannot be verified from the given information.
  SC: an action before its prerequisite (object not held or not received yet, use before pass, put down
      before pick up); a no-op or circular step.
  CQ: required collaboration missing; a wrong target robot or object; a duplicated, circular or unfinished
      request; collaboration that is not needed.
- Unverified success is not success: do not assume an unreported action, movement or hand-over happened.
- Do not give credit for style, length, confidence or good intent.
Give a short reason for every score that lists the defects found (or states that there are none).
