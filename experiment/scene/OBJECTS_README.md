### 속성 요약 (물건 목록)

- **openable** (열기/닫기): Blinds, Book, Box, Cabinet, Drawer, Fridge, Kettle, Laptop, LaundryHamper, Microwave, Safe, ShowerCurtain, ShowerDoor, Toilet
- **toggleable** (켜기/끄기): Candle, CellPhone, CoffeeMachine, DeskLamp, Faucet, FloorLamp, Laptop, LightSwitch, Microwave, ShowerHead, Television, Toaster
- **moveable** (밀기/당기기, 가구류): ArmChair, Bed, Chair, CoffeeMachine, CoffeeTable, Desk, DeskLamp, DiningTable, Dresser, FloorLamp, GarbageBag, GarbageCan, HousePlant, LaundryHamper, Microwave, Ottoman, Safe, ShelvingUnit, SideTable, Sofa, Stool, TVStand, Television, Toaster
- **breakable**: Bottle, Bowl, CellPhone, Cup, Egg, Laptop, Mirror, Mug, Plate, ShowerDoor, ShowerGlass, Statue, Television, Vase, Window, WineBottle
- **dirtyable** (더럽히기/닦기): Bed, Bowl, Cloth, Cup, Mirror, Mug, Pan, Plate, Pot
- **sliceable**: Apple, Bread, Egg, Lettuce, Potato, Tomato
- **cookable**: Potato
- **속성 전혀 없음**: Curtains, Painting, Sink
